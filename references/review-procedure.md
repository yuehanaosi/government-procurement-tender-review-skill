# 审核程序与输出模板 / Review Procedure and Output Template

## 一、建立审核底稿 / 1. Build the review record

以最新确认的采购文件及补遗为基准，抽取明确的资格条件、无效响应情形、评分标准、格式要求、实质性参数和合同条件。记录版本、包号、条款、适用阶段以及证据页。遇到 PDF 加工、分卷或补页，重建页码映射。多人或多供应商同批审核时，各自维护证据表。

Use the latest confirmed procurement documents and addenda to extract eligibility rules, grounds for rejection, scoring criteria, formatting requirements, material specifications and contractual terms. Record the version, lot, clause, stage and evidence page. Remap pages after conversion, splitting or insertion. Keep a separate evidence record for each supplier.

## 二、核对重点 / 2. Review checks

| 检查项 / Check | 证据和判断 / Evidence and decision |
|---|---|
| 主体与资格 / Entity and eligibility | 对照营业执照、许可证、资格声明和采购公告，核对主体、有效期、联合体及分包约束、资格证据的法定替代方式。/ Match licenses and declarations to the notice. Verify entity, validity, consortium and subcontract restrictions, and permitted alternatives for evidence. |
| 授权、签字与印章 / Authority, signatures and seals | 查看实际页面，按条款核对签署身份、授权范围和生效日期、每个明确要求的签名栏及本单位章。区分发证机关印章、供应商印章、扫描签章和经验证的电子签章。可见图章与密码学验签的证据分别记录。/ Inspect the rendered page for signer identity, scope and effective date of authority, required signature fields and the supplier seal. Distinguish issuing-agency seals, supplier seals, scans and validated electronic signatures. Record visual and cryptographic evidence separately. |
| 保证金和期限 / Security and timing | 对照收款方或受益人、供应商、项目编号、金额、提交方式、提交时间和有效期。分别核对响应有效期、担保责任期间和履约期。只有可核对的平台记录才作为实际递交证据。/ Verify payee or beneficiary, supplier, project, amount, mechanism, timing and validity. Check response validity, the security coverage period and performance period separately. Use verifiable platform records as evidence of submission. |
| 报价 / Pricing | 按行复算数量、单价、合价，检查含税口径、币种、分包限价、报价版本、大小写和一览表与分项表。遇到不一致，保留原值并引用适用修正规则。异常低价按适用规则审查并要求相应证据。/ Recalculate every line and subtotal; verify taxes, currency, lot ceilings, versions, numeric and written totals, and agreement across forms. Preserve original figures and cite any correction rule. Follow applicable rules and evidence requirements for abnormally low bids. |
| 实质性响应 / Material requirements | 对照品目、型号、数值、数量、供货范围、交付、验收、服务期和分包限制。用证书型号、附件及配置表交叉核对。区分明确负偏离、证据不足、OCR错误和非强制差异。/ Compare items, models, values, quantities, scope, delivery, acceptance, service period and subcontract restrictions. Cross-check certificate models, schedules and configurations. Separate demonstrated deviations, missing evidence, OCR errors and nonmandatory differences. |
| 优惠和评分 / Preferences and scoring | 核对中小企业、国内产品及绿色采购政策的适用条件，并核对声明、采购标的、行业、主体和数据。逐项映射业绩、人员、证书和其他评分证明。按采购文件判断属于资格、强制性要求、价格评审优惠或得分。/ Verify applicability of SME, domestic product and green procurement policies, then check declarations, items, industry, entities and data. Map references, personnel and certificates to scoring criteria. Determine whether each condition concerns eligibility, mandatory compliance, price preference or scoring. |
| 文字与版本 / Wording and version | 查错字、日期、文号、单位、项目名称、同类表述冲突及模板残留，区分采购方原模板与供应商填报错误。图像和文本提取不一致时复查页面图像。/ Check typographical errors, dates, document numbers, units, project names, conflicting expressions and placeholders. Identify whether an error originated in the template or supplier entry. Reopen the rendered page when text extraction differs from the image. |

## 三、处理方式 / 3. Handling findings

截止前给出可执行的修改或核验动作。截止后，评价可由有权主体启动的澄清、说明、更正或其他程序，并核对所适用方法的限制。将算术修正、歧义解释与补交实质性材料分开判断。结论使用“已确认”“需核验”或“当前材料无法核验”，只有条款和证据均明确时才表述为明确不符合。

Before the deadline, provide actionable corrections or verification steps. After the deadline, consider clarification, explanation, correction or other procedures initiated by the authorized body and verify the limits for that procedure. Assess arithmetic correction, clarification of ambiguity and supplementation of material evidence separately. Label each finding confirmed, pending or unverifiable from the supplied records. State definitive noncompliance only when both rule and evidence support it.

Verify electronic government procurement and electronic construction tendering under their respective rules and platform instructions.

## 二、全国地方检索 / 2. Local lookup nationwide

从[中国政府采购网 / China Government Procurement Network](https://www.ccgp.gov.cn/)检索项目编号及地方分网，从[全国公共资源交易平台地方入口 / National Public Resources Trading Platform directory](https://www.ggzy.gov.cn/platform/platform.html)进入项目交易地平台。再核对本级财政、发展改革和行业主管部门的政策公开目录。平台服务规则用于确认交易操作，政策适用应查正式发布主体、适用对象和正文条款。

Search China Government Procurement Network by project number and locate the local subsite. Use the national trading platform directory to locate the project platform. Cross-check official policy registers of the responsible finance, development and reform, and sector authorities. Use platform guidance for transaction steps and verify policy applicability against its issuer, scope and operative text.

项目所在市、区、县的规定和采购公告，按项目预算级次、主管权限及公告指定平台另行定位。对于适用不同制度的项目，参考[香港政府招标资料 / Hong Kong SAR government tender information](https://www.fstb.gov.hk/tb/tc/business/gov_procurement/information-on-government-tender.htm)和[澳门公共采购网站 / Macao SAR public procurement portal](https://www.pcp.gov.mo/)，根据当地制度独立核验。

Locate relevant municipal, district and county provisions according to budget level, supervisory authority and platform named in the notice. For projects under separate regimes, consult the Hong Kong SAR or Macao SAR sources and verify the rules independently.

### 地方核验示例 / Examples of local verification

- 青海可从[青海省财政厅规范性文件目录 / Qinghai finance department policy register](https://czt.qinghai.gov.cn/zfxxgk/gz/XWGL_ZC_XZGFXWJ.html)检索集中采购目录及标准，按项目年度核实是否有更新。/ In Qinghai, locate the centralized procurement catalogue and thresholds in the official policy register and check for subsequent versions applicable to the project.
- 西藏可对照[自治区政府采购工作指南 / Tibet government procurement guide](https://czj.lasa.gov.cn/czj/c100342/202511/f4a53d05c4184c81977fa5fd97f453af.shtml)和[自治区公共资源交易平台 / Tibet public resources trading platform](https://ggzy.xizang.gov.cn/)，分别查项目程序和线上递交。/ In Tibet, use the procurement guide to locate procedural sources and the regional platform to verify electronic submission requirements.

地方发布机构、文号、链接、适用区域、预算级次、有效日期和项目条款应随审核底稿归档。重点查集中采购目录与限额、公开招标数额标准、电子签章、保证金、公告更正、异议或质疑渠道及行业特别规则。发现地方要求和全国制度疑似冲突时，记录条款与项目相关性，交由有权主体确认。

Retain the local issuer, document number, link, territory, budget level, effective dates and project clause. Focus on catalogues and thresholds, open-tender thresholds, electronic signatures, bid security, corrigenda, challenge channels and sector rules. Record apparent conflicts with national rules and refer the question to the responsible authority.
